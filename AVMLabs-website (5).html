ABOUT AVMLABS

A lab built around one instrument nobody talks about: honesty.

AVMLabs is a diagnostic testing service built on a simple premise — every test on this list runs on validated, automated instrumentation, and every price on this list is the price you actually pay. No hidden handling fees, no "price varies," no fine print between the rate card and the bill.

THE PHILOSOPHY BEHIND THE PRICE LIST

Built on a scientist's conviction that testing shouldn't be a luxury.

Dr. Velumani spent over a decade as a research scientist at BARC before turning his training in thyroid biochemistry into a business, founding Thyrocare Technologies in 1996. Over the next two decades it grew into one of India's largest diagnostic chains, built on a then-unconventional idea: centralise processing into one highly automated lab, and pass the savings on as lower prices rather than higher margins.

That same hub-and-spoke, volume-first logic is what AVMLabs carries forward — accurate, lab-grade diagnostics, priced for repeat use rather than one-off emergencies.
