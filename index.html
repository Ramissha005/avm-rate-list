ABOUT AVMLABS

A lab built around one instrument nobody talks about: honesty.

AVMLabs is a diagnostic testing service built on a simple premise — every test on this list runs on validated, automated instrumentation, and every price on this list is the price you actually pay. No hidden handling fees, no "price varies," no fine print between the rate card and the bill.

01

Precision

Samples are processed on calibrated Photometry, E.C.L.I.A, H.P.L.C, Immunoturbidimetry and Hematology platforms — the same instrument classes used across India's leading reference labs.

02

Access

One rate card for our B2B partners — clinics, hospitals and collection centres — showing your cost, the patient-facing B2C price, and the margin on every test. No negotiation needed.

03

Automation

Fewer manual handoffs between your sample and your report means fewer places for error to creep in, and faster turnaround on results.
