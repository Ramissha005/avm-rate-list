SARC

Services At Reagent Cost

Truth. Trust. Transparency.

SARC is how AVMLabs prices testing at, or near, the cost of the reagent itself — not a discount, a different cost structure. Higher volume and better utilization push the effective cost per test down far enough that reagent cost becomes the number that matters, while the business stays sustainable on overheads and scale rather than on markup.
