SARC

Services At Reagent Cost

Truth. Trust. Transparency.

SARC is how AVMLabs prices testing at, or near, the cost of the reagent itself — not a discount, a different cost structure. Higher volume and better utilization push the effective cost per test down far enough that reagent cost becomes the number that matters, while the business stays sustainable on overheads and scale rather than on markup.

HOW SARC WORKS

Five factors bring cost down to reagent cost

None of these alone gets a test to reagent cost — combined, they compress the effective cost per test until overheads stop dominating the price and the reagent itself does.

📈

Higher Testing Volumes

More tests run on the same analyzer and the same fixed overheads spread across far more results — the cost each individual test carries keeps shrinking.

🧪

Better Reagent Utilization

Larger, steadier batches mean less reagent lost to calibration, dead volume and short-dated stock — closer to the manufacturer's quoted cost per test, not above it.

⚙️

Analyzer Utilization & Economies of Scale

An analyzer running more hours a day earns back its own cost faster — the same machine, staff and floor space serving many more billable tests.

💰

Reduced Effective Cost per Test

Volume, utilization and reagent efficiency compound together — the real, fully-loaded cost of a single test keeps falling as throughput rises.

🔧

Operational Efficiency

Standardized workflows and less manual handling per sample cut the labor and error cost riding on top of every result.
