ABOUT

CENTRAL REFERENCE LABORATORY

The CRL will house the best of technologies and analysers that can offer a 1,500+ test menu, that have less volume but needs higher end technologies, including LC-MS, ICP-MS, GC-MS, NGS and NMR, in addition to all conventional laboratory technologies routine laboratories use.

In simple words a 100+ Low End, High Volume and Low Value tests will be done Locally in Local Processing Laboratories (LPL). While a 1000+ High End, Low Volume and High Value tests will be done in Centralised Reference Laboratory (CRL) which will serve to many countries that cover 3 Billion Population.

By Centralising rare menu, localising routine menu, optimises menu, cost, speed and quality for entire range of tests in clinical chemistry. Thus AVMLabs entire menu Affordable, Accessible, and Reliable for every Pincode in this country.
